# VIT FoodHub - Project Overview

## 1. Introduction

VIT FoodHub is a campus food ordering and management system designed to make food ordering more convenient for students and improve the management of food orders for campus food outlets.

The system provides a digital platform where users can browse available food items, select items, manage their cart, place orders, and track their orders.

## 2. Project Purpose

The main purpose of VIT FoodHub is to reduce unnecessary waiting time at food outlets and provide a more organized food ordering experience within the campus.

## 3. Objectives

The major objectives of the project are:

- Provide students with an easy way to browse food options.
- Allow users to place food orders digitally.
- Reduce queues and waiting time at food outlets.
- Provide an organized order management process.
- Improve communication between customers and food outlets.
- Maintain a structured system for managing food-related information.

## 4. User Roles

### Student

Students can:

- Browse available food items.
- View food shop menus.
- Add food items to the cart.
- Modify their cart.
- Place orders.
- View order information.
- Track their orders.

### Shopkeeper

Shopkeepers can:

- View incoming orders.
- Manage available food items.
- Update order status.
- Manage shop-related information.

### Administrator

Administrators can:

- Manage users.
- Manage food shops.
- Monitor the overall system.
- Manage system-related information.
